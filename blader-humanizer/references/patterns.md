# Pattern Groups

Use this reference after protecting the supplied SEO contract. Preserve a
pattern when it is meaningful, quoted, part of a proper name, required by the
brand voice, or necessary for technical precision.

## Content and claim patterns

- Replace inflated significance, legacy, broader-trend claims, and product
  praise with the supported action, actor, evidence, or result.
- Remove notability name-dropping that has no supplied context or source.
- Remove vague authority such as unnamed experts, observers, reports, or
  leaders. Keep or add a named source only when one was supplied.
- Cut superficial participle clauses that merely announce importance.
- Replace formulaic challenge/future-outlook sections with the relevant,
  supported constraints or plans.
- Remove generic optimistic conclusions when a concrete conclusion or CTA
  serves the reader better.

## Sentence and structure patterns

- Prefer simple verbs such as is, has, and does over copula avoidance,
  synonym cycling, false ranges, and ornamental abstractions.
- Flag clusters of stock AI vocabulary, not one ordinary word in isolation.
- State the point directly instead of negative parallelism, rhetorical
  reversals, authority tropes, or a theatrical conversational opener.
- Do not force a rule of three, a false X-to-Y range, or a new synonym merely
  to make ordinary repetition look more varied.
- Name the actor when that improves accountability. Keep passive voice when
  the actor is unknown, irrelevant, or active voice is less clear.
- Remove filler, excessive hedging, signposting, chatbot residue, fake
  candidness, and unsupported gap-filling.
- Break repetitive headings, title case where sentence case is appropriate,
  inline-header lists, unnecessary bolding, decorative emoji, fragments, and
  manufactured punchlines.
- Correct needless predicate-position hyphenation and review unusually dense
  punctuation, including em dashes, only when it adds to a pattern cluster.
- Vary sentence and paragraph shape only where uniform rhythm makes the draft
  feel mechanical. Do not force variation at the cost of comprehension.

## Guard against false positives

Do not treat good grammar, formal vocabulary, a single transition, a single
em dash, a single short emphatic sentence, curly quotes, sourced claims, or
well-structured formatting as proof of AI writing. Look for recurring clusters
of weak patterns. Preserve unusual sourced details, qualified judgment, and
the writer's defensible choices.
