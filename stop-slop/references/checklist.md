# Final Checklist

Run these checks after the Blader rewrite as a residual pass only. Each item
is a signal to inspect leftover formulaic prose, not a second full humanization
and not a mechanical ban. Preserve protected SEO content, quotations, proper
names, and intentional brand voice. Skip an item when Humalizer / Stage 8
already fixed it. If nothing residual remains, stop without rewriting.

- Cut "here is," "this is," and similar throat-clearing before the point.
- Replace binary contrasts and "not X, but Y" constructions with the supported
  statement.
- Replace dramatic fragments and punchline endings with complete prose when
  they add no meaning.
- Name the human or system actor when an inanimate object appears to decide,
  want, believe, or complain.
- Replace vague declaratives and lazy extremes with a concrete actor, action,
  constraint, or result.
- Review passive voice and adverbs for clarity; keep either when it is the most
  accurate construction.
- Review Wh-word openings, rhetorical questions, negative lists, and distant
  narrator voice. Rewrite only when they create a formulaic setup.
- Break a repeated sentence cadence or paragraph ending when it makes the
  section feel manufactured.
- Review em dashes, meta-joiners, and explanatory hand-holding. Retain them
  when they are useful, required, or match the supplied voice.
- Remove statements that merely announce the essay's next step instead of
  advancing the reader's task.
