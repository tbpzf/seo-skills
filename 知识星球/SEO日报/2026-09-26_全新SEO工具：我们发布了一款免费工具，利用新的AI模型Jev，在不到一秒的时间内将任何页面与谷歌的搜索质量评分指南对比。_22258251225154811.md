为什么这对SEO很重要
 - 谷歌的质量评级员不会给你定排名。他们的指南描述了谷歌排名系统旨在奖励的内容：第一手经验、真实专业知识、信任，以及能满足页面目的的内容。未达标的页面往往会随着时间推移失去优势。

优质评审员Jev通过以下方式帮助内容和SEO团队：
- 在毫秒级内对每个质量评级测试评分
- 标记评级者可能发现的E-E-A-T和内容质量缺口
- 允许优先考虑高收入但评分较低的页面
- 展示模型在每次通过或失败时的信心

# Rate a webpage against *Google Quality Raters Guidelines*

Enter a URL. The report opens on its own page with the rating, every judgment behind it, and what still needs a person.

02How it works

## Three steps, *all visible.*

Render

#### Browser Run reads the page

A headless browser loads the URL as a visitor would and returns the page as Markdown. Code pulls out facts: bylines, About and Contact links, dates, ad markers, AI boilerplate.

Judge

#### Jev answers the rubric

One request asks thirty-odd typed questions drawn from the guidelines: purpose, YMYL, harm and spam flags, main content quality, E-E-A-T, website information, and Needs Met when you give a query. Each answer comes back as a level with probabilities.

Rate

#### Code applies the rules

Harm, deception, and spam force Lowest first. A firm Low attribute gives Low. High and Highest need the guideline's High criteria. YMYL raises scrutiny. Every rule that fired is listed under the rating.

#### What it cannot see

- Reputation. The guidelines require research beyond the page; the report tells you which searches to run.
- Layout, images, and ad placement. Ads and obstruction are judged from the text only.
- Other pages on the site, so systemic problems can only be suspected.
