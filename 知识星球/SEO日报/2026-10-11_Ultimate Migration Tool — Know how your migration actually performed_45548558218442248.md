Ultimate Migration Tool — Know how your migration actually performed

一个免费的网站迁移复盘工具：接上 Google Search Console(只读权限),把换域名、换 CMS、改版前后的数据按上线日对齐比较，看迁移到底掉没掉、掉在哪。

要点：
1. 只看 4 个数：点击、曝光、CTR、平均排名。前后数据按上线日逐日对齐叠成一张图，另有标出上线日的真实日历时间线。
2. 口径值得抄:CTR 用总点击 ÷ 总曝光，不把各页 CTR 再求平均;平均排名按曝光加权，避免低流量页把均值拉偏;http/https、带不带 www 当成不同 URL,不合并。
3. 用法：谷歌账号登录 → 上传"旧 URL → 新 URL"映射表 CSV(不传就做域名级对比)→ 采数，每次迁移最多 5,000 个 URL → 出 4 份报告：总对比、关键词(哪些词涨了、跌了、消失了)、逐页重定向、迁移健康看板(恢复预测 + 风险分)。
4. 可以导入 Screaming Frog 抓取结果，按映射表逐条核对重定向链;报告能导出 CSV 和 PDF。适用于换域名、.co.uk 转 .com、改 CMS、改版、URL 重构、HTTP 转 HTTPS。

## What it measures

There are four numbers every migration pretty much boils down to - clicks, impressions, click-through-rate and average position. The Ultimate Migration Tool helps you to see how your migration has performed, whether that's a domain migration, CMS change or a reskin. The handy timeline shows you the progress of your website migration side-by-side so that you can easily see the before and after.

### CTR

Real aggregate: clicks ÷ impressions, never an average of averages.

### Position

## How it works

The Ultimate Migration Tool runs on our server, with read-only access to your Google Search Console for accurate analysis.

### 1. Connect to Tool

Sign in with Google. We use read-only access only as this tool can never modify a property, and your credentials never reach the browser.

### 2. Map URLs

Upload a CSV of the old URL to the new URL. Skip for a domain-level comparison, or add it for page-by-page accuracy and full migration analysis.

### 3. Collect Data

Wait for a few moments while we gather the data from Google Search Console. Currently limited to maximum of 5,000 URLs per migration.

### 4. Migration Analysis

You get 4 migration reports: main comparison, keyword performance, page-by-page redirects & a migration health dashboard.

## Works for any kind of migration

Not just for domain changes, the Ultimate Migration Tool analyses anything that alters your URLs or shifts your Google Search Console data.

- **Same-domain changes**  
  Site reskins, CMS migrations, URL restructures, or an HTTP to HTTPS move, it's all tracked with the same tool.

- **Domain-to-domain migrations**  
  A ccTLD move like .co.uk to .com, or a full rebrand from one company domain to another.

- **Exact URL matching**  
  http vs https and www vs non-www are treated as genuinely different URLs.

- **Verified, not guessed**  
  Redirects are checked against a real crawl import, and CTR is a true aggregate, not an average of averages.
