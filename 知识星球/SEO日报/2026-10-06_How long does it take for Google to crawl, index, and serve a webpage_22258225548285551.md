## 星球正文

How long does it take for Google to crawl, index, and serve a webpage?

Estela Franco 在巴塞罗那的 Google Search Central Live 活动上记下了 Gary Illyes 的几张幻灯片:Google 抓取、索引、展示各个环节平均要花多久。这类时间 Google 平时很少一次性公开。

要点:
1. 抓取:新页面平均约 20 小时被抓;已知页面平均约 30 天重抓一次;sitemap 平均约 24 小时处理。
2. 索引:渲染从几秒到几小时;meta 信息处理平均约 45–90 分钟;链接信息处理从几分钟到 1–3 周;整个索引流程平均约 1.5 小时;删除页面平均 1–3 周;改版迁站平均约 1–3 个月。
3. 展示:在 Search Console 里提交移除平均约 2 小时生效;搜索结果摘要更新 1–2 天;文本结果里的图片更新约 1–2 周;核心更新后的恢复平均 3–6 个月。

How long does it take for Google to crawl, index, and serve a webpage?

I attended the [Google Search Central](https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/googlesearchcentral/?trk=public_post-text) Live Deep Dive Europe event organized by [Gary Illyes](https://ch.linkedin.com/in/garyillyes?trk=public_post-text) and [Cherry Sireetorn Prommawin](https://sg.linkedin.com/in/cherry-prom?trk=public_post-text) in Barcelona this week.

There were tons of talks and information, but I'd like to share some details. Although experience gives us a rough idea of the timelines, Google doesn't usually share this level of detail in just a few slides.

🕷️ Crawling:
- A new page: ~20 hours on average
- Refresh (known page): ~30 days on average
- Sitemap processing: ~24 hours on average

📚 Indexing:
- Render the page: seconds to hours
- Process meta annotations: ~45-90 minutes on average
- Processing link annotations takes an average of minutes to 1-3 weeks.
- End-to-end indexing: ~1.5 hours on average
- Removal: 1 to 3 weeks on average
- Site move: ~1-3 months on average

💻 Serving:
- Removal on SC: ~2 hours on average
- Snippet update: 1-2 days on average
- Text result image update: ~1-2 weeks on average
- Core update change (recovery): 3-6 months on average
