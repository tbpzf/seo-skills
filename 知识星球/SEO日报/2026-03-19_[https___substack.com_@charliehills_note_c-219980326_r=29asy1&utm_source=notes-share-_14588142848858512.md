LinkedIn experts charge $500+ for profile optimisation.

Google Gemini does it for free:

Step 1: Download your profile

- Go to your LinkedIn profile.

- Click More → Save to PDF.

Step 2: Set up Gemini

- Open Google Gemini Pro.

- Enable Canvas and "Thinking".

Step 3: Upload and prompt

- Upload your profile PDF.

- Paste this prompt → charliehills.substack.c…

- Answer the 6 questions provided.

Step 4: Get your strategy brief

- Gemini rewrites your entire profile.

- New headline, about, and experience.

Step 5: Refine in Canvas

- Delete what you don't like.

- Select sections and click Extend.

Step 6: Generate your visuals

The prompt generates 4 image prompts:

- Banner (1584 x 396)

- Profile picture (400 x 400)

- Featured tile 1 (552 x 368)

- Featured tile 2 (552 x 368)

Click "Create image" and paste them in.

Upload your headshot as reference.

- Scroll down to the 4 prompts.

- Copy the banner instructions.

Step 7: Apply to your profile

- Download the images generated.

- Upload them to your LinkedIn.

Full profile rebuild with zero design skills.

Repost ♻️ to help someone in your network.
