# Research a target guest-post host

Use this branch in Step 1 whenever the user names a target publication or
provides its website. The target URL and supplied keywords are enough to begin;
find guidelines and reader evidence independently. A user-provided guidelines
URL is a lead to verify, not a substitute for checking the current host site.

## Locate the editorial rules

Visit the target site and find its first-party contributor, guest-post, pitch,
editorial, sponsored-content, and disclosure pages through navigation, site
search, or a domain-restricted search. Follow the applicable path for the
requested placement. Record source URLs and the date checked. Extract only
rules the host actually states: accepted topics, intended readers, pitch or
submission process, length and format, originality and rights, author bio,
sources, AI-assisted writing, product mentions, links, and disclosures. If
sources conflict, prefer the applicable current first-party rule and flag the
conflict for review.
Treat third-party summaries and patterns in published articles as clues, not
binding contributor rules.

Keep a rule check for each material requirement: the verified rule and its
first-party URL and check date, or `unknown` with the pages/searches checked.
Include topic fit, length/format, citation policy, originality/rights,
AI-assisted writing, product mentions/links, disclosure, byline, and
pitch/submission process.

## Observe the readers

Check the host's About or audience page, relevant sections or categories, and
several recent articles close to the user's keywords when available. Use author
or contributor pages and reader comments or questions when available. Record
who the host explicitly serves, what tasks and depth its articles address, how
similar topics are framed, and what a useful new contribution would add. Cite
the pages behind each observation and label inspected support `checked`.
Keep user-supplied evidence `supplied` until inspected. Label a reader situation inferred from coverage as a
`hypothesis`; mark absent or inaccessible audience evidence `unknown`.

Pass the sourced rules, audience observations, hypotheses, gaps, and supplied
keyword inventory to `seo-audience-strategy` in `single-content brief` mode.
The parent guest-post route decides the final angle and publication status.
If pages cannot be found or accessed, record what was checked and which
requirements remain unknown, then continue with a provisional brief or draft.
