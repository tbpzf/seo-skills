# Distinctiveness audit

Apply the [helpful-content standard](helpful-content.md).
Review the opening, every major section, proof, examples, and next action.
Record the passage or section, reader job, missing or weak material, and a
concrete repair. Run a critical walkthrough of the promised task using only the
draft and its verified artifacts; identify where a reader must guess, seek an
omitted answer, or trust unsupported advice.

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Reader change | The piece helps the target reader decide, act, or understand something specific |
| Core contribution | A supported contribution improves the baseline answer in a way that matters to the promised task; novelty claims have an inspected comparison |
| Section value | Each major section answers a distinct question, teaches an action, shows evidence, or explains a trade-off |
| Source honesty | Material claims match their exact reference and evidence status; first-hand voice, results, quotes, and authority match their actual source and approval |
| Evidence limits | Numbers, comparisons, outcomes, and product claims carry scope, method, and caveats |
| Concrete detail | The reader has the actions, inputs/outputs, examples, artifacts, and difficult decisions needed to complete the promise; one isolated detail cannot carry a thin how-to |
| Judgment | Facts, reasoning, recommendation, relevant alternative, and conditions are inspectable; caveats or counterexamples change advice where appropriate |
| Coverage | The packet's material items appear in the planned sections or their omission is explained |
| Reader respect | The draft reaches the useful answer quickly and avoids obvious setup, inflated lists, and empty promises |

Rank findings by whether they weaken the central promise, then by reader
usefulness. A humanization or grammar pass may improve prose, but it cannot
repair a missing source. Put `proof needed` and other editorial requests in the
parent's audit, outside publishable copy. Omit unsupported optional claims;
return a publication blocker when essential support for the promise is missing.
