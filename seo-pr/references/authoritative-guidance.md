# Authoritative press-release guidance

Use this synthesis to draft and audit SaaS releases. It records practical
guidance from major press-release distribution services, accessed August 4,
2026. Treat platform limits as distribution-specific requirements and the
shared principles as the default house standard.

## Shared standard

The sources converge on these rules:

1. Lead with actual news. A product release must say what is new, who it serves,
   when and where it is available, and why the change matters.
2. Use a specific, concise headline and a strong first paragraph that covers the
   five Ws. Put the most important information first.
3. Keep an objective news tone. Avoid advertising language, vague superiority,
   jargon, keyword stuffing, long sentences, and filler.
4. Support claims with attributable facts, data, product specifications,
   certifications, methodology, or approved customer evidence.
5. Use quotes for interpretation and significance, not to repeat the headline.
   Identify speakers and their relevant roles clearly.
6. Finish with a concise company boilerplate and a public media contact.
7. Add relevant multimedia with descriptive captions. Give journalists a clear
   next step and supporting links.
8. Keep names, dates, titles, product terminology, and company details
   consistent across the release, newsroom, website, and media assets.

## Platform details and reconciled defaults

PR Newswire recommends a headline around 75-100 characters when possible and
describes 300-500 words as a typical AP-style release length. Business Wire
allows a much larger 250-character headline, but that is a platform maximum,
not a writing target. Use 75-100 characters as the default preference; exceed
it only when accuracy or a required proper name demands it.

Business Wire identifies the headline, dateline city, and media contact as core
submission elements, requiring at least a public email and/or phone number for
the contact. It advises against links in headlines and recommends captions for
multimedia. Apply these checks even when another distributor is used, then
verify that distributor's current submission rules before sending.

PR Newswire recommends AP-style structure for US releases: headline, dateline,
lead, body, quotes, boilerplate, and media contact. Use market-appropriate style
and datelines for international distribution rather than copying US formatting
unchanged.

## SaaS translation

For SaaS announcements, translate the shared rules into concrete verification:

- Replace “innovative platform” with the product name, new capability, actual
  workflow, target user, availability, and supportable effect.
- Distinguish launch, beta, early access, preview, general availability, and a
  roadmap commitment. Never collapse these states.
- State supported plans, regions, integrations, inputs, outputs, and limits only
  when approved. Treat security, privacy, compliance, AI accuracy, and ROI
  claims as evidence-sensitive.
- Prefer a product leader's explanation of the customer problem or design
  decision over a generic executive celebration quote.
- Link to the product page, documentation, methodology, demo, or press kit that
  helps a reporter validate and explain the news.
- Recommend a changelog or blog post instead when the update lacks significance
  outside current users.
