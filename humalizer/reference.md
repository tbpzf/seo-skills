# Reference notes and SEO examples

## Sources and adaptation

- [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) is a prompt-only skill: a Markdown instruction file pairs a large catalog of AI-writing patterns with voice calibration and a draft → audit → rewrite loop. Its stated source is [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing).
- [hardikpandya/stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop) uses a smaller core rule set and progressive disclosure: its `SKILL.md` contains core checks and points to phrase, structure, and before/after reference files. It uses a five-dimension review score before delivery.

Humalizer adopts the useful mechanisms, not their text or rigid style bans:

1. Pattern-based diagnosis before rewriting
2. Optional voice calibration from real user samples
3. A separate audit after the first rewrite
4. Focused, one-level-deep reference material
5. A score that triggers revision rather than pretending to detect authorship

For SaaS SEO, a blanket “no passive voice,” “no adverbs,” “no em dashes,” or “no lists of three” rule is counterproductive. It can break product terminology, change legal or technical precision, and make prose sound mechanically altered. Judge those choices in context.

## SEO-specific before and after

### Empty product praise

**Before**

> AcmeFlow is a powerful, game-changing platform that transforms the modern marketing landscape.

**After**

> AcmeFlow lets lifecycle marketers build an approval workflow for campaign emails and publish the approved version from one workspace.

The revision states a supported capability and audience. It must still be checked against product documentation.

### Keyword repetition

**Before**

> CRM software for startups helps startups choose CRM software that supports startup sales teams. The best CRM software for startups makes startup CRM management simple.

**After**

> Startups need a CRM that gives a small sales team a shared view of pipeline, customer history, and next steps. Evaluate setup time, data imports, reporting needs, and the workflows your team will use every day.

The phrase can remain in the title, H1, opening, and a relevant heading. The body earns relevance by answering the task.

### Vague proof

**Before**

> Industry leaders trust AcmeFlow to unlock measurable growth.

**After**

> [Add an approved customer quote or a sourced case-study result here.] Until then, explain the workflow without a trust claim.

### Formulaic transition

**Before**

> This is not just a reporting tool. It is a new way to unlock insights.

**After**

> The reporting workspace lets revenue teams filter pipeline by owner, stage, and source before exporting a weekly review.

### Generic CTA

**Before**

> Ready to transform your business? Get started today.

**After**

> Start a free trial to build your first pipeline report with your own data.

The CTA describes a concrete post-click action. Only use it if the trial actually supports that workflow.

## Review checklist

Use this checklist after the main audit:

- Is the opening answer or promise specific to the reader and query?
- Can every superlative, statistic, customer name, and result be verified?
- Does each product mention demonstrate a capability relevant to the current section?
- Do headings communicate content rather than repeat a keyword?
- Are citations, internal links, and the CTA still present and accurate?
- Did the revision preserve caveats, limits, and technical terms that affect a buying decision?
