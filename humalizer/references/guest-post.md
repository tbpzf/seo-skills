# Guest-post rewrite branch

Use when `seo-guest-post` calls Humalizer for a new article or revision. Work
from the article, evidence-labeled reader brief, supplied keyword decisions,
publisher guidelines, contributor relationship, and approved claim sources.
The parent retains the in-memory article and owns submission notes and any
user-requested file save.

Protect the host's title and format rules, exact required headings, byline, disclosure, citations,
quotes, source context, verified claims, links and anchor restrictions,
originality terms, and any required wording. Preserve the contributor's
supported experience without inventing first-hand scenes or opinions. Keep
each selected keyword where it serves the reader; record a variant or omitted
term for the parent's final reconciliation. Follow the host's voice and reader
knowledge rather than an owned-site sales voice. A guest article needs no
product CTA unless the host permits it and the reader has a useful next step.

Preserve the contribution's actual method, criteria, examples, and caveats, along
with its source-to-section mapping. Reuse an adequate packet for a supported
prose edit. Headings and section order may change within the host's format rules
when the authorized edit improves comprehension; report material changes. Check
that the revised article still supports the reader's promised task or decision,
and keep evidence gaps in submission notes rather than the article.

Humanization is a prose review and rewrite, not an AI-authorship detector or
proof of human authorship. If the host forbids AI-assisted submissions, return
that conflict to the parent as a publication blocker; do not claim the rewrite
makes the article compliant.

Return the complete revised article without an audit wrapper, plus a separate
list of material changes and any claim, rule, keyword, or disclosure conflict.
The stage is complete when the parent can compare every material change with
the host contract and continue to the final prose and grammar checks.
