# SEO指标比大多数人想象的更容易伪造。

- 发布时间：2026-09-29T12:17:40.655+0800
- 作者：虾虾
- 星球原帖：[查看帖子](https://wx.zsxq.com/group/88882458825452/topic/45548584541448488)
- 标签：#SEO#

## 星球正文

SEO指标比大多数人想象的更容易伪造。

Dmytro Sokhach 做了个实验：给一个根本不存在的网站刷 DR，看看这个指标到底有多好操控。

要点：
1. 2023 年第一次测：域名都没注册，没流量、没内容、没排名、没真实外链，唯一目标就是把 DR 刷上去。
2. 两年后重测：在 Fiverr 随手下了一单，不到 100 个引用域名、不到一周，DR 就被刷上去了。帖子附的 Ahrefs 截图里，这个站 DR 69，引用域名 61 个，有机关键词 0，在 AI 回答里被引用 0 次。
3. 作者自己也说，这种外链没有任何 SEO 价值，带不来流量，也带不来排名。

结论：DR 高不等于网站强。判断一个站的时候要看流量、排名这些实际表现，别只看 DR。

    What is Domain Authority?

    You’ve probably seen it come up in SEO reports…
usually as a number out of 100.

    But what does it actually mean?

    Domain Authority is basically a score that estimates how strong your website is compared to others.

    It’s influenced by things like:

    how many websites link to you
how trustworthy those sites are
how established your website is

    Sounds important, right?

    Here’s the part most people don’t realise…

    It’s not a Google metric.

    It’s created by tools like Moz to estimate how likely you are to rank.

    So while it can be helpful as a comparison tool…
it’s not something Google is directly looking at.

    And this is where people get stuck.

    They focus on increasing a number,
instead of focusing on what actually drives results.

    Because you don’t need a high Domain Authority to get enquiries.

    You need:

    the right content
the right structure
and the right people landing on your site

    Domain Authority can give you context.

    But it’s not the goal.
