01 / The problem

## AI search is a black box. Your rank tracker can't see inside it.

/ 01

### The engine rewrites your keyword

ChatGPT doesn't run your keyword. It rewrites it into its own searches, fetches pages you never see, and works out on its own who gets mentioned.

/ 02

### Position three is worth nothing

Rank tracking measures a results page your buyers open less every year. Position three is worth nothing if the answer never mentions you.

/ 03

### Read the answer's own data

FanoutFox reads the answer's own data. You get the queries it wrote, the pages it fetched, the ones it cited and the brands it mentioned.

06 / FAQ

## Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid tier or trial counting down. I built it because I wanted it to exist.

Store or manual install?

Both work and both are the same code. The [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fanoutfox-ai-answer-geo-i/efgemipiccaljhahdacjbbkcemelojlj) is one click and it updates itself, so start there. The catch is that ChatGPT changes its internal format often, sometimes twice in a week, and every store update waits in Google's review queue for a day or three. When a format change breaks captures I can put the fix on the [download page](https://fanoutfox.com/download/) the same day. The store copy catches up later. Keep that page bookmarked for those days.

Does my data leave the browser?

No. It all runs on your machine. Captures sit in your browser's own storage and are never uploaded. Two optional lookups make the only outbound calls, Ahrefs domain ratings and Reddit thread stats, and you can switch both off.

Which engines does it support?

ChatGPT, and only ChatGPT. Perplexity capture was retired in v2.0.0 and Gemini isn't planned. ChatGPT changes its internal format constantly, and keeping up with one engine properly is better than keeping up with three badly. Sessions you captured on Perplexity before that still open in your Library.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation. It's the work of getting your brand picked, cited and mentioned inside AI answers instead of ranked on a results page. It's what SEO becomes when the answer replaces the results page.

Do you see my ChatGPT login?

No. It uses the session token your browser already holds, once, to fetch your own conversation, then throws it away. It's never stored or sent anywhere.

Will it slow ChatGPT down?

No. It does nothing at all until you click the fox. It never intercepts, proxies or changes your conversations.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes. Capture the answers for a client's queries, pin them, run them again later and compare who entered or dropped out. Plenty of people use it as a light GEO rank tracker across several client brands.
