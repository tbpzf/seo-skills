## 星球正文

Content Effort: One of Google’s Signals Your SEO Agency Doesn’t Want You to Know About

Ann Smarty 认为,Google 在打击的不是 AI 写的内容本身,而是"低投入(low-effort)"的内容。能不能被检测出是 AI 写的、内容有没有用,都不是关键;关键是你有没有给这篇内容加进别处没有的东西。

要点：
1. Google 搜索质量评分员指南(QRG)里 "Effort" 这个词出现了 120 次,定义是:一个人为了做出让人满意的内容,实际投入了多少工作。指南里被判为最低投入的例子,就是那种只把别人的内容换个说法、不给出处、没有专家观点的文章,作者说这正是很多站在发的典型 AI 文章。
2. Google 官方的有用内容文档里也写了 Effort:写原创分析、手工翻译一首诗、给游戏做一张自定义互动地图算高投入;从 feed 自动生成页面、用生成式 AI 大量产文却没有人工审核把关,算几乎零投入;标注出处不能代替原创投入。
3. 作者的区分:"非同质化内容"是调研、自有数据、白皮书这类权威资产,一年做不了几篇;"有投入的内容"是全站所有内容(教程、测评、产品对比)的编辑标准。投入不等于字数、不等于内容深度(AI 也能做调研)、也不等于你花多久调 prompt。
4. Shaun Anderson 在 Google 泄露的算法文档里找到一个 contentEffort 属性,作者说目前不知道它是不是直接排名信号、影响有多大,但它存在本身就说明问题;有人认为它支撑了 Helpful Content 更新。

结论：真正要问的是,Google 或大模型凭什么要把这篇展示出来?你加了什么新东西,让它比同主题的一大堆内容更好?

# Content Effort: One of Google’s Signals Your SEO Agency Doesn’t Want You to Know About

### Is Google starting a war against AI-generated content? Yes, because it lacks effort and originality.

Scaling content production has accelerated since the ChatGPT launch. Businesses and publishers are actively saving money on content creation by generating content using generative AI.

There are lots of tools claiming their AI-generated content is “undetectable” and “high-quality”.

Google’s saying, “We don’t care how your content was produced as long as it’s useful,” has been perceived as “Go ahead, and generate your content with AI”.

**But is it really the case? All signs point to the opposite.**

**>>>> [JOIN us to discuss this topic LIVE and bring your questions!](https://www.linkedin.com/events/7512905618597289984/)**

Long-term SEOs had already known this cycle: SEO agencies that like profit margins will rush to AI-generate content for their clients → AI-generated content will dominate the web → **Google will act. And this is what is already happening as we speak**.

So far, everyone who cared to listen and watch has seen lots of signs that Google is actively penalizing AI-generated content:

- There are lots of case studies showing websites generating content using AI are being penalized by Google’s Spam Update every year. @Lily Ray has [lots of great examples](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/it-works-until-it-doesnt-ai-content-risks) in her newsletter.

- There’s a brand [new patent](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-research2023-media/pubtools/1039292.pdf) on ~~AI content~~ “*AI slop*” detection.

- Google has just updated its official [documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) mentioning that any AI-generated OR AI-assisted content should be labeled as such.

All of this was done right before Google’s Spam update, which was longer than any other Spam update so far. I’ve been in SEO long enough to know all of this points to one thing: Google is starting a war against low-effort AI-generated content, and the key here is “**low-effort.**”

[SVG ImageSVG Image](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7sGF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcf3fa1b4-826c-41b8-8e50-bca6c9511856_1608x640.png)

**And I said this before: [If something is too easy to do, it is neither valuable nor is going to result in long-term growth](https://www.annsmarty.com/p/the-only-true-seogeoreddit-strategy).**

Because everything valuable comes with hard work, and it is not about engineering a sophisticated prompt to generate content. *This is a popular argument by those in favor of AI-generated content (and currently in denial that it won’t work)*.

You may think you outsmarted Google and came up with a genius prompt on how to generate your article, but in the end you are publishing recycled low-effort content, and Google doesn’t like rewarding low-effort.

## Content effort and SEOSVG Image

[SVG ImageSVG Image](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_YKZ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0aa2f2d4-222b-4367-840a-af4e034fdb33_666x486.jpeg)

Content effort is not at all a new concept in SEO.

“Effort” is mentioned 120 times in the [official guidelines](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf) used by human raters who evaluate Google’s search results. Here’s how content “effort” is explained there:
> Effort: Consider the extent to which a human being actively worked to create satisfying content.

Here’s an [example](https://guidelines.raterhub.com/images/EzQsJxcdkWWPeLj5M9WN.jpg) of an article that the quality raters’ guidelines encourage to classify as **the lowest-effort**:

[SVG ImageSVG Image](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0nUA!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Faf8b5622-d54c-437f-bda1-b57dfe66939d_1424x1656.png)

While the content itself is relevant, it simply repurposes other people’s content without providing any sources, adding any expert opinions, or otherwise showing any effort in creating it.

**This is a classic AI-generated article that many website owners publish on their websites.**

And it is the lowest-rated page, according to Google’s guidelines.

@Cyrus Shepard, who used to work as Google’s quality rater, shares that a lot of attention was paid to content effort during training, and it was always easy to tell low-effort content from high-quality content. He [shares some examples](https://signal.zyppy.com/p/content-effort):

[SVG ImageSVG Image](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!d6Ym!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F49c7f61d-0fc7-4961-a67f-74366ddd7bc8_1266x536.png)

At this point, content effort is also part of Google's official [guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content). Read it twice if that helps:
> ***Effort:** The extent to which human work went into creating the content or the systems powering it. For example, writing original analysis, translating a poem manually, or building a custom interactive map for a game represents high effort. Conversely, automatically generating pages from feeds or using generative AI to produce large amounts of text without manual oversight or curation represents little to no effort. Attribution or giving credit to other sources doesn’t replace the need for original effort.*

Note: I already shared a similar concept of [“non-commodity” content earlier](https://www.annsmarty.com/p/non-commodity-content-what-seos-and). To me, the distinction is as follows:

- “non-commodity” content = your authority-building / [data-driven digital PR asset](https://www.smarty.marketing/home/infographic-link-building/) that helps you become the source (surveys, proprietary data, whitepapers). No business can produce too many of these a year.

- “content with effort” = your overall editorial strategy for ALL the content that exists on your site (how-tos, reviews, product comparisons, etc.)

Repeat after me:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!IIWj!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9b8b1884-8eeb-48fa-8abc-d6f210c44e8f_220x220.jpeg)

**When it comes down to AI-generated content, it doesn’t matter if it can be detected as AI. It doesn’t even matter if it is actually useful.**

- Effort is NOT the number of words

- Effort is NOT depth of content (AI can do research well)

- Effort is NOT the amount of time you were refining your prompt to generate “high-quality content in your voice”

- **Effort is how much time YOU (or your team) dedicated to the piece to make it truly original (this is key!) because the web is already oversaturated with repurposed content.**

**What really matters is: WHY would Google or LLMs surface it? Is there anything new YOU added that is worth being found? How is it better than a ton of similar content on that topic???**

## Content “effort” as part of the algorithmSVG Image

@Shaun Anderson [found](https://www.hobo-web.co.uk/what-is-googles-content-effort-signal/)*[contentEffort](https://www.hobo-web.co.uk/what-is-googles-content-effort-signal/)*[attribute](https://www.hobo-web.co.uk/what-is-googles-content-effort-signal/) in the Google algorithm leak back in 2025. We don’t know if it is a direct ranking signal or how much it can impact a website’s visibility, but the fact that it exists in the algorithm is very telling.

The attribute is believed to power the [Helpful Content updates](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/helpful-content-per-googles-latest-guidelines) and was later refined enough to become part of the core algorithm updates.

So ignoring (or faking) the content effort concept may decrease your visibility algorithmically.

Unfortunately, I’ve seen a lot of (even big) brands switching to AI-generated content production, and low-effort is pretty obvious there.
