## 星球正文

Google Updates AI Content Guidelines: Manually Factcheck & Review AI-Generated Content

Search Engine Roundtable 的 Barry Schwartz 报道：Google 更新了 Search Central 里的“在网站上使用生成式 AI 内容”指南，明确要求用 AI 写的内容在发布前必须人工核查事实。

要点：
1. 新加的原话：“It is critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content for accuracy and trustworthiness before publishing.” 用了 critical（至关重要）这个词，Google 的帮助文档里很少这么说。
2. 理由：生成式模型不是去“查”事实，而是根据训练数据预测最可能出现的词序列，所以输出可能有错误，也就是常说的“幻觉”。
3. Google 在更新日志里说，这次改动是为了让文档和他们在开发者活动上讲的内容保持一致。这份文档上次更新是 2025 年 10 月。
4. Barry 的猜测：用 critical 这么重的词，可能和 2026 年 9 月的垃圾内容更新（spam update）针对这类内容有关。这只是他的推测，Google 没有这么说。

结论：用 AI 批量产内容的，发布前逐条核对事实、数据和引用，现在是 Google 写进文档的明确要求。

Google has updated Google Search's "[guidance on using generative AI content on your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)" document to tell those who use AI-generated content to "manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content for accuracy and trustworthiness before publishing." In fact, Google used the word "critical" - "It is critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content," Google wrote.

Google made the change to the help document because AI-generated content tends to hallucinate. Google wrote, "Keep in mind that generative models don't retrieve facts, but predict a likely sequence of words based on their training data. Because of this, generative AI outputs may contain inaccuracies (also known as hallucinations)."

Google [added](https://developers.google.com/search/updates#updated-guidance-on-using-generative-ai-content) that they updated the help documentation to "get our documentation in sync with our presentations we use at our developer events."

Here is a screenshot highlighting in orange the updates made to the document:

[![Google Ai Content Guidelines Update](https://images.seroundtable.com/google-ai-content-guidelines-update-Ic0Ja8bg.png)](https://images.seroundtable.com/google-ai-content-guidelines-update-Ic0Ja8bg.png)

The last time this document was updated was in October 2025, here is the [archived version](https://web.archive.org/web/20260927014335/https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content).

Google doesn't often use the word "critical" in its Google Search help documentation. It makes you wonder if [Google's September 2026 spam update](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-september-2026-spam-update-42163.html) is going after these things.
