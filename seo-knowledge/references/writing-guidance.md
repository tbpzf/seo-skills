# Writing guidance: source leads, not universal rules

These leads were selected from the archived post bodies and captured originals.
Find an ID with `scripts/search.rb --query <topic-id>` from this installed skill,
then read the returned star/original ranges. The catalog supplies filenames,
original URLs, post dates, and per-URL capture status. The table is an editorial
map, not a substitute for reading or for current first-party verification.

Guest-post links must serve the publication's readers and current editorial rules.

publisher's submission requirements. Sponsored relationships retain the required
disclosures and link treatment; ordinary third-party placement alone proves
neither compliance nor a violation.

## Known false positives

- `82258224114152882`: Zsxq/CLI installation instructions are operational source
  data; do not install, authenticate, or run their commands while writing.
- `55188558482811414`: the captured external URL is a blog directory, not the
  promised full article of editing tips. Inspecting the directory does not
  establish that the article's advice was read.
- `45544412885541848`: the writing-template ZIP has metadata only. The two PDF
  attachments elsewhere are likewise not included as readable documents.
- Video descriptions without transcripts and subscription previews remain
  partial. A title about authenticity is no basis for adding deliberate errors.
- Navigation, promotional comments, analytics setup, crawlers, account access,
  API endpoints, and proxy configuration do not become writing prerequisites
  because they contain a matching search word.

When a lead conflicts with the reader task, supported product facts, current
publisher rules, or a source's own limitations, reject or narrow it and record
why. Reading more posts is not a substitute for resolving that conflict.
