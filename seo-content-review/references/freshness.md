# Check freshness and feature changes

Use when product facts or reader instructions may have changed. Check
applicability before recommending edits; age alone is not a defect.

## Establish the change

Start with the user's change notes and inspect the relevant release notes,
current documentation, approved fact sheet, or product files. Record:

- Previous statement or workflow, and new supported behavior.
- Exact source and supporting passage or artifact; evidence status.
- When the change takes effect, release status, and relevant version, plan,
  region, role, platform, rollout, or beta restrictions.
- Check date separately from source-update date and effective release date.

A newer page does not automatically settle a conflict. Check whether the old
statement still applies to the article's audience, version, or plan. Withhold
unsupported replacements when sources disagree or release scope is unknown;
identify the smallest fact needed to resolve the conflict. A checked product
repository change may show implementation without proving production rollout.

## Follow dependent passages

For each confirmed change, inspect the supplied article for dependent claims
and recommendations. Relevant places include the opening and main promise,
steps, screenshots or UI labels, examples, feature limits, pricing or plan
eligibility, integrations, alternatives, FAQ, conclusion, title/meta description,
internal links, and CTA destination. Check only the assets and pages accessible
within the request; name unavailable ones.

| Changed fact | Current source and scope | Dependent passage or asset | Reader impact | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old behavior → supported new behavior | Exact reference; date/version/plan | Section or quoted phrase | What becomes misleading or unusable | Correct / qualify / remove / retain / verify |

A negative claim such as “direct import is unavailable” may now need correction
and a revised decision rule, not an extra feature paragraph. A feature available
only to some readers needs that qualification wherever eligibility affects the
answer. Broaden the promise only when the user's update scope and evidence
support it; keep unrelated sections and the reader's original task intact.

## Preserve context

Current product guidance should describe supported current behavior. Historical
case studies, dated announcements, release notes, or accounts of an older
experiment remain accurate in their original context. Recommend a dated
clarification or current-reference link when useful instead of rewriting the
past. Do not invent a new result, quote, screenshot, or tested workflow.

Change a “last updated” date only after an actual substantive revision and in
accordance with the user's publication convention. A review check date can be
recorded in the report even when no content changes. Publication and deployment
remain actions for the owning route under the user's instructions.

The freshness check is complete when changed facts are traced to every relevant
dependent passage in the supplied scope, unaffected or historical statements
have a retain reason, and unresolved applicability has a verification action.
